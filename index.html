How I work

Three habits I bring to every product

Picked up from shipping real software weekly, and from an engineering degree where a wrong number has physical consequences.

01

Work back from the question

Before writing code, I write down the questions users actually ask. At Tellyflow I designed the database schema backwards from the answers the business team needed.

02

Define success before building

An AI feature without a metric is a demo. I set up repeatable evaluations that score runs against expected results and track pass rates release to release.

03

Trust is a feature

Reliability and privacy come before new features: least-privilege access, no surprise data storage, and graceful failure instead of crashes.

Prospect Intelligence

Internal AI product

Problem

The business team needed to know what 15–20 target companies were hiring for, announcing and prioritising. The answers were buried across 200+ unstructured public documents.

Users

What I did

  • Designed the PostgreSQL schema backwards from the answers users need.

InboxIQ: AI email triage

Personal product · 2026
Built end to end

Problem

Small support teams lose time sorting a shared inbox. Urgent complaints sit next to newsletters, and every reply starts from a blank page.

Users

Small businesses running customer support out of a Gmail inbox.

What it does

Connects to Gmail and uses an LLM to label each email with one of 8 categories and 3 urgency levels, plus a one-line summary and a suggested reply.

Product decisions

  • User trust as a requirement: read-only Gmail access, encrypted tokens, email bodies never stored, all data deleted on request.
  • Never trust raw model output: every response is checked against a strict schema, with a safe fallback.
  • Guardrails in code, not just the prompt: no drafted replies to automated senders.

Problem

AI agents look impressive in demos but fail quietly in use. Without a measurement, nobody can tell whether a new version is better or worse.

Approach

I defined success first: a repeatable evaluation that scores each run against expected results and tracks pass rates across versions.

What I'm building

A workflow that splits multi-step tasks across specialised agents, with tools exposed through an MCP server.

Guardrails
